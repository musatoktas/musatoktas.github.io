Biography

In short

I am Musa Toktaş, a computer engineer based in Dubai.

I am a BSI Associate Tutor. BSI has approved me to deliver its ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS and ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AIMS training courses, and I have delivered courses on behalf of BSI to a range of clients. I work with BSI as an associate under contract.

For seven years I have led technology, product and compliance work in companies in travel, health tourism, SaaS and aerospace consulting, in the UAE, Europe and Turkey. I was CTO of HOI Holding, a 70-person health tourism group. I founded Heraklet, an engineering and certification consultancy for aerospace, defense and safety-critical systems, and MBD Group, whose brands include MBD Travel. I led the Guide of Dubai app, an AI-driven tourism platform.

In each of these roles I was responsible for the technology and for its security: risk assessments, access control, data protection under GDPR and UAE PDPL, and readiness for ISO/IEC 27001 audits.

Earlier I worked as a software engineer at Siemens. Today I also work on TypeScript performance: I wrote whyts and send fixes to open source projects.
